Systems thinker

I lead with clarity. Good products start with content, not components: what does the user need to know now, next, and later?

Featured work

Systems design

Navigation overhaul

I created a single, consistent header and footer, with personalization built in and designed to work on any tech stack, for a portal that sees 110,000 daily visitors. Teams each own a piece of the portal on their own stack, and until this launched, that meant users met nine different headers, three with no way back to home. I won buy-in from product leaders to build it as a global component. It launched on schedule and was adopted by a majority of product areas.

Product design

Homepage vision + redesign

I set the product vision and designed a scalable student portal homepage that breaks a large amount of personalized information into small, prioritized pieces: what’s urgent first, then what’s next. I created a hierarchy for every type of message and tested it with users over 14 studies and production tests. Moving alerts inline saw about twice the engagement of pop-ups and changed how the portal delivers messages. 82% of users in the initial opt-in test chose the new experience over the existing one.

From hub to help

I turned an overlooked resource hub into active work and coached the designer, who reshaped it into a system for finding help. His idea put resources inside the tools students already use and was designed so search, agentic chat, and references all started with a single source of truth.
